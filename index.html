The problem

Most building disputes begin before the first bag of cement.

Building a house is probably the biggest spend of your life. Many owners sign and send a down payment before they check three things.

✕Is the license real?Is the contractor's PCAB license current, and does the name match your contract?
✕Is the land yours to build on?Tax declaration only? Title in deceased parents' names? Agricultural land?
✕Did you pay for real progress?Does each payment match work that was actually inspected?
